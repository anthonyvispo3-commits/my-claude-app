Standing corrections — carried from the SOP

  • A13 CapCut exports video, not audio. Render the cover-plus-audio piece as MP4 (H.264, 1080 px); the .WAV master from Ableton is what goes to DistroKid.
  • A12 DistroKid needs raster art — PNG or JPG at exactly 3000 × 3000 px, RGB, under 10 MB. Keep the Canva .SVG as the editable copy only.
  • B2 The QR code needs a live release URL, so it cannot close on release day. Generate QR codes on a commerce day for whatever went live since the last one.
  • A3 Start the Mureka generation before opening Word, so the full 30 minutes of lyric editing runs over it.
  • B1 Three colourways only — White, Black, Brand Purple (PMS 2665 C). No POD partner matches that Pantone on a blank, so choose each one's nearest purple from a physical sample, not the on-screen swatch.
